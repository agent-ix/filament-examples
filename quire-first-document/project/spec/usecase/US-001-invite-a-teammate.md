# US-001: Invite a teammate with a link

## Story

**As a** team owner
**I want** to send a teammate an invitation link
**So that** they can join my team without me creating an account for them.

## Context

Owners invite people by email. An old invitation that still works is a risk, so an invitation should stop working after a short time.
